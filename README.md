# West Side Dojo

Operations home for West Side Martial Arts & Boxing Academy.

## What goes here
- Class schedules, coach notes, and facility plans
- Grant applications and drafts (never submit without explicit approval)
- Nonprofit paperwork and 501(c)(3) tracking
- Financial records for the gym (separate from personal and Apricot Odyssey funds)

## Filing rules
- Dojo money stays in dojo records; never mix with personal or Apricot Odyssey funds
- Grant drafts are drafts until approved for submission
- Private only, always.
